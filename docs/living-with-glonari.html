Two ways to own

When You’re Ready, Choose What You Own.

Own the Home

Buy the house itself as ordinary real property, with its future appreciation. You can keep using Glonari services as a member.

Own the Complete Glonari Home

Buy the house together with its established Home Business: its Gia setup, its Global Connections relationship, its operating history and the opportunity to keep running it. Your share of what it creates is yours to use however you choose.

You don’t have to decide on day one. That’s the point.
