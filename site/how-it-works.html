Chapter 02 · Where the value comes from

The Money Comes From Real Activity. Not From Living in the House.

Your Home Business earns from real commercial relationships, coordinated through Global Connections. Because Glonari works with thousands of homes, it can negotiate deals that no single family could get on its own.

Household savings

Better rates on insurance, utilities, maintenance, services and everyday purchases.

Merchant and service relationships

Commissions and rewards from partners you choose to use.

Referrals

Introducing people to Glonari Homes, or businesses to the network, when it fits.

Your knowledge

Reviews, research, local insight and experience that companies value, shared only with your permission.

Gia finds the opportunity. Your participation creates the value.

Chapter 04 · How results work

The More Value You Create, the More You Receive.

Your Home Business doesn’t pay a fixed amount. Each month, it receives a share of the qualifying value created through your participation. A more active month can produce more. A quieter month produces less.

Part of that value supports your home while you live there. The rest belongs to you. Use it for savings, travel, education or anything else. Glonari doesn’t require you to spend it on housing.

Results are not guaranteed and depend on actual qualifying activity and program terms.

05

Chapter 05 · GLO: the power behind Gia

GLO Powers Your Gia. You Create the Value.

Running an AI-powered Home Business takes real technology: Gia’s AI capacity, Global Connections, Digital Banker and the Glonari Home platform. GLO™ is your Glonari membership and access credential. It gives you and your home access to that capacity.

What GLO is

  • Your membership
  • Your access to Gia’s AI capacity
  • Your qualification for Glonari Home services

What GLO is not

  • Rent
  • A down payment
  • A share of the property
  • A source of income

Note: The GLO level for each home depends on the home and program tier. Gia will show you what applies during qualification.

Chapter 06 · Your monthly statement

Complex Behind the Scenes. Simple for You.

Digital Banker™, Glonari’s financial management experience, puts everything about your home in one place: what you paid, what your Home Business created, what supported your home and what is yours.

Ask Gia: “Why was my share higher this month?” Gia explains the activity behind every number.
